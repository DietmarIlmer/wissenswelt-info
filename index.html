Über Minerva

Minerva ist ein Lexikon mit 202 Artikeln aus 17 Bereichen, von Religionen über Medizin bis Technik. Durch die App begleitet dich Minerva, die Wissenseule. Die App ist werbefrei und funktioniert ohne Konto. Alle Artikel und die tägliche Runde sind kostenlos; „Minerva Plus“ bietet zusätzliche Funktionen gegen Bezahlung.

So entstehen die Inhalte

  • Die Artikel stützen sich auf anerkannte Quellen: Fachgesellschaften, Behörden, Statistikämter, Museen, Fachliteratur und wissenschaftliche Übersichtsarbeiten. Die wichtigsten Quellen stehen unter jedem Artikel bei „Quellen & Stand“.
  • Zahlen, Rekorde und Ereignisse geben den Stand des angegebenen Datums wieder und können sich ändern.
  • Umstrittene Themen werden ausgewogen dargestellt; wo die Forschung uneinig ist, steht das dabei.
  • Die Texte wurden mit Unterstützung künstlicher Intelligenz erstellt und redaktionell geprüft. Trotz Sorgfalt können Fehler vorkommen. Bitte melde sie über „Fehler melden“ – wir korrigieren schnell.

Wichtiger Hinweis

Die Inhalte dienen der Allgemeinbildung. Sie ersetzen keine ärztliche, psychologische, rechtliche oder finanzielle Beratung. Bei gesundheitlichen Beschwerden wende dich an eine Ärztin oder einen Arzt.

About Minerva

Minerva is an encyclopedia with 202 articles across 17 areas, from religions and medicine to technology, with Minerva the owl of knowledge as your guide. It is ad-free and works without an account. All articles and the daily round are free; “Minerva Plus” offers extra features for a fee.

How the content is made

  • Articles are based on recognised sources: professional societies, public authorities, statistical offices, museums, specialist literature and scientific reviews. The key sources are listed under each article in “Sources & date”.
  • Figures, records and events reflect the stated date and may change.
  • Controversial topics are presented in a balanced way; where research disagrees, this is stated.
  • The texts were created with the help of artificial intelligence and editorially reviewed. Despite great care, mistakes can happen. Please report them via “Report an error” – we fix them quickly.

Important note

The content is for general education. It does not replace medical, psychological, legal or financial advice. If you have health problems, please see a doctor.

Privacy

In short: Minerva does not collect personal data.

  • There is no account, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics or tracking tools and no cookies for advertising or statistics.
  • Your progress (articles read, saved list, points, streak, settings) is stored only locally on your device in browser storage (“localStorage”). It is not transmitted to us or to third parties. You can reset it at any time in the settings or delete it via your browser settings.
  • The read-aloud feature uses your device’s speech output, which may be provided by the device or operating system manufacturer; Minerva itself does not transmit any data.
  • No external fonts, scripts or third-party content are loaded. Links to sources open in your browser only when you tap them.
  • The app runs entirely on your device. There is no server of ours it exchanges data with, and it needs no internet connection – except for purchases via Google Play.
  • Purchases (Minerva Plus) are handled entirely by Google Play. You enter payment details only with Google; we receive neither your name nor payment details. The app only asks Google Play whether Minerva Plus is active and stores this status on your device.

  • Daily reminders are scheduled locally on your device. For this the app asks for permission to send notifications; you can turn them off at any time in the app or in Android settings.
  • Share features open your device’s share menu. You decide what to share and with whom; the app transmits nothing itself.
  • If you email us (e.g. “Report an error”), we use your message only to handle your request and delete it afterwards.
